# Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, profitability, product performance, and geographic sales trends. The dashboard enables business users to monitor key KPIs, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## Business Problem

Retail organizations generate large volumes of sales data across products, categories, and regions. Without proper analytics, it becomes difficult to identify high-performing products, understand profitability drivers, and monitor business performance.

This dashboard was built to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Total Orders
* Product Performance Metrics

---

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Overview

Provides a comprehensive view of business performance through:

* Revenue, Profit, and Profit Margin KPIs
* Product-level sales analysis
* Geographic sales distribution
* Profit contribution by category
* Profit trend analysis over time

![Sales Overview](01_Sales%20Overview.png)

---

### 2. Key Influencers Analysis

Uses Power BI's AI-powered Key Influencers visual to identify factors that drive higher sales volume.

Key findings include:

* Product categories influencing quantity sold
* Product attributes impacting sales performance
* Comparative analysis of influential factors

![Key Influencers](03_Key%20Influence.png)

---

### 3. Decomposition Tree Analysis

Enables users to perform root-cause analysis by drilling down from Total Revenue into multiple business dimensions.

Analysis includes:

* Revenue by Country
* Revenue by Product Category
* Revenue by Customer Segments
* Interactive hierarchical exploration

![Decomposition Tree](02_Decomposition%20tree.png)
